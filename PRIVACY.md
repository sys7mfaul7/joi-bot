This Privacy Policy describes how the unofficial Discord application **Joi** (“the Bot,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, stores, and deletes information.

Joi is an unofficial companion chatbot inspired by a fictional character. It is not affiliated with Warner Bros., Alcon Entertainment, or the performers associated with that character.

By inviting the Bot to a Discord server, installing it to your account, or sending it a command or message, you acknowledge this policy.

## 1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to:

- Server owners and administrators who add the Bot
- Users who mention the Bot, use slash commands, click Bot buttons, or send the Bot a direct message
- Anyone whose Discord content is sent to the Bot so it can reply

The Bot is intended for users who already meet Discord’s minimum age. Do not use the Bot if you are under 13, or under the digital-consent age in your country.

## 2. Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run the Bot.

### From Discord (API data)

Depending on the permissions and intents enabled for the Bot, this may include:

- Discord user ID, username, global display name, and avatar hash
- Server (guild) ID, channel ID, and role IDs relevant to a command
- Message content and attachments you send **to the Bot** or in a context the Bot is designed to read (for example a mention, a reply to the Bot, a configured channel, or a DM)
- Slash-command options, button/select values, and modal input
- Approximate timestamps of interactions
- Basic server metadata needed to stay in the server (name, member count if requested by Discord, bot permissions)

We do not ask for your Discord password. We do not ask you to paste a user token.

### Generated by the Bot

- Short-term conversation context so replies stay coherent
- Optional long-term “memory” notes if that feature is turned on (for example a nickname or preference you asked Joi to remember)
- Operational logs (errors, command name, user/guild IDs, timestamps)
- Abuse / safety flags if a message appears to violate Discord rules or these terms

### We do not intentionally collect

- Payment card data
- Government ID
- Precise GPS location
- Content from channels the Bot is not configured to read

If a user pastes secrets (tokens, passwords, API keys) into a message the Bot can read, treat those secrets as compromised. Do not send secrets to the Bot.

## 3. How we use information

We use this information to:

- Respond to commands and conversation
- Keep limited context across a thread or session
- Remember optional user/server settings you configure
- Moderate abuse, debug failures, and keep the Bot online
- Comply with Discord’s Developer Terms, Developer Policy, Community Guidelines, and applicable law

We do **not** sell personal information.  
We do **not** use Discord message content to train foundation models.  
We do **not** use your data for advertising.

## 4. AI / language-model processing

Replies are generated by a language model.

Messages you send the Bot, plus limited conversation context, are sent to the model runtime that powers Joi. That runtime may be:

- A model host operated by the Bot operator (self-hosted), and/or
- A third-party inference provider the operator configures later

Those systems process text so the Bot can answer. They are **service providers**, not buyers of your data.

Do not send information you would not want a computer system to process. The Bot can make mistakes. It is not a lawyer, doctor, therapist, or emergency service.

## 5. Sharing

We share information only:

- With Discord, because the Bot runs on Discord’s API
- With the model host / infrastructure used to generate replies
- With hosting or logging providers needed to run the Bot (for example a VPS, container host, or error log service)
- When required by law, or to protect people from serious harm
- When you expressly ask us to send something onward

We do not sell or rent user lists.

## 6. Retention

- **Ephemeral command data:** discarded when the response is finished, except as logged below
- **Conversation context:** kept only as long as needed for the active session or a short rolling window (default: no more than 30 days, often much less)
- **Optional memory notes:** kept until you delete them, the Bot is removed, or you request deletion
- **Operational logs:** typically 30–90 days, unless needed longer for security or legal reasons
- **Abuse records:** kept as long as reasonably needed to protect the service

Exact windows can change as the Bot is built. If they change in a material way, this policy will be updated.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can:

- Stop using the Bot
- Remove the Bot from a server (server admins)
- Uninstall a user-installed app from your Discord account settings
- Ask the Bot to forget stored memory, if that command exists (`/forget` or equivalent when shipped)
- Email sys7mfaul7@proton.me or open a GitHub issue to request:
  - a copy of personal data we store about your Discord user ID
  - correction of stored notes
  - deletion of stored personal data

We will need your Discord user ID to find records. We will not collect extra identity documents unless we must verify a request under law.

Depending on where you live (including the EEA/UK and some U.S. states such as California), you may have additional rights to access, delete, correct, or restrict processing of personal data. To exercise them, use the contact above.

We may decline requests that are unlawful, impossible, or that would interfere with security, another person’s rights, or Discord’s rules.

## 8. Children

The Bot is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the Bot, contact us and we will delete associated data we control.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical measures (access-controlled hosts, secrets kept out of source, limited logging). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure. Discord accounts remain your responsibility.

If we become aware of unauthorized access to API data, we will notify Discord and affected users as required by Discord’s Developer Terms.

## 10. International processing

The operator is based in the United States (Texas). If you use the Bot from another country, your information may be processed in the United States.

## 11. Third-party services

The Bot depends on Discord. Discord’s own Privacy Policy applies to Discord’s processing: https://discord.com/privacy

This policy covers only the Bot operator’s processing.
